THE DOG ON THE DIRT ROAD
Characters: Walter, a skinny old man in a straw hat and white vest. A brown pit bull.

Panel 1
A long empty dirt road under a hot sun. Walter walks barefoot along it.
CAPTION: Walter walked this road every day for forty years.

Panel 2
Close-up of Walter's bare feet in the dust.
The frame focuses on countless grains of sand collecting around his toes.

Panel 3
A pit bull behind a rickety fence barks furiously at Walter.
DOG: WOOF! WOOF! WOOF!

Panel 4
Walter keeps walking, staring straight ahead, completely unbothered. Walter says, "Morning."

Panel 5
Wide shot. The dog keeps barking as Walter disappears into the distance.
CAPTION: Every day, the same conversation.

Panel 6 the following afternoon. The same dirt road. The pit bull is lying against a wall, looking exhausted.

Panel 7
Walter walks past again. The dog lifts one eye.
WALTER: Long day?

Panel 8
The dog sighs. Close-up on its tired face.
CAPTION: Even rivals need a day off.
